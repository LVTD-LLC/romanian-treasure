THE WORKING VERDICT

We have not found a hidden reserve.
We have found a better trail to investigate.

The strongest inspected research points to gold remaining in Soviet accounting beyond the revolution, then being absorbed or dispersed in the early 1920s. The next useful discoveries are likely to be inventories, transfer vouchers and receiving records. Identifiable cultural objects need a separate search.

This is a provisional synthesis of published evidence—not an independent audit of the decisive original Russian files. Inspect the checkpoint · Inspect the disposal report
